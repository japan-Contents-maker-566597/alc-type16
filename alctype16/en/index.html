What is the Drunk Personality Test?

Drinking behavior is a mirror of your hidden personality.

This test isn't just about measuring your physical alcohol tolerance.
By combining psychological approaches with behavioral pattern analysis, we reveal what role you naturally take in group settings and how you communicate through the medium of drinks in 16 unique personalities.

Knowing your own and your friends' "Drunk Personality" will spark great conversations and help you create a more fun drinking environment! 🍻

The 4 Axes of Drunk Personalities

Our algorithm analyzes your drinking style based on 4 psychological dimensions.

L/F

Leader / Follower

Determines if you take the initiative at parties or follow the group's flow.

O/R

Oozappa (Rough) / Restrict

Measures your balance between partying wildly and acting responsibly (schedule & budget).

U/D

Upper / Downer

Shows whether you become hyper and energetic or quiet and sentimental.

T/Y

Tsuyoi (Strong) / Yowai (Weak)

Represents your physical alcohol tolerance and typical drinking pace.
