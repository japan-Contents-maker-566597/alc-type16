The 4 Personality Axes

This quiz analyzes your relationship with alcohol across 4 distinct axes.

  • L/F

    Leader / Follower

    Do you want to steer the night, or go with the flow?

  • O/R

    Outgoing / Regimented

    Do you prioritize fun and the moment, or do you keep track of the last train and the bill?

  • U/D

    Upper / Downer

    When drunk, do you get louder and more energetic, or quieter and more introspective?

  • T/Y

    Tough / Yielding

    Can you handle lots of drinks, or does it hit you fast?
