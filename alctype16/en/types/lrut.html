16 Drunk Personalities

Your drunk personality is...

The Vibe Curator (LRUT)

Plans everything down to the minute. High energy, perfectly orchestrated. The pro event manager who approaches every night out like a major production.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The High-Resolution Producer
For you, a night out isn't just "hanging" — it's a critical project. You obsess over venue selection, seating arrangements, flow, energy management, and even the routing to the second bar. You find joy in controlling the experience, and the results speak for themselves. You're what a "functional adult who knows how to have fun" actually looks like.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The Scheduling Demon
You're constantly checking the time. "Last orders in 5 — everyone decide!" You're high energy, but your eyes are always locked on the landing. When the bill comes, you're the one who's maximized every loyalty point and optimized the payment method. Nobody asked. Nobody had to.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: The Specialist of Smooth Execution
With you around, there are no awkward "where should we go?" delays, no forgotten orders. You're a professional maximizer of everyone's enjoyment. Your hospitality and execution make you an invaluable asset in any social setting.

Growth Tip

【Tolerance for Chaos】
The best moments of any night out are the unplanned ones. Try letting the schedule slip a bit and seeing where the chaos takes you. Surrender is sometimes the move.

Best Match

· FRDT (The Group CFO): You lead the front, they handle the back-office. Together you create the most seamless and error-free night out imaginable. The ultimate tag-team.

Worst Match

· LOUY (The Blackout Time Bomb): LOUY passes out at bar one, destroying your entire timeline. Their existence is a scheduling bottleneck you cannot plan around.
