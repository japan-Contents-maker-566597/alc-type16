Your drunk personality is...

The Group CFO (FRDT)

Not flashy, not loud — but the entire success of the night quietly rests on your shoulders. The bill, the math, the lost phone. The unsung backbone of every great night out.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Logical Back-Office Support Pillar
You don't seek the spotlight, but without you the night collapses. You handle the check, sort the cleanup, manage the forgotten items, and arrange transport home — steadily, precisely, without making a scene. Alcohol doesn't dull your accuracy or your sense of responsibility. You are a quiet professional, operating at full capacity all night.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The Ledger-Keeper's Stoic Conversation
As the night winds down, you slip away to the register and sort the payment before anyone else has noticed. Your split calculations are always fair, your point redemptions are always optimized, and your follow-up payment request message the next morning is a work of precision and speed. Nobody else does this. Nobody thinks to.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: Unmatched Operational Excellence and Trust
With you around, money disputes don't happen. Items don't get forgotten. The night lands cleanly. You are not glamorous, but you are indispensable. You are the infrastructure of the group. Without you, everything would be slightly worse in ways nobody could quite explain.

Growth Tip

【Taking Credit】
It's okay to drop a "you're welcome" once in a while when you've handled the bill again. People genuinely appreciate what you do. Let yourself receive that acknowledgment — you've earned it.

Best Match

LRUT (The Vibe Curator)

LRUT runs the front of house. You run the back office. Together you build the most seamless, error-free night out possible. An unbeatable partnership.

Worst Match

LOUY (The Blackout Time Bomb)

Lost wallets, lost phones, forgotten tabs — LOUY creates work that drains your carefully managed resources and tests your patience significantly.
