Your drunk personality is...

The Blackout Time Bomb (LOUY)

Starts the party HARD, but their body can't keep up. Goes from being the life of the party to a crash-and-burn legend in the first bar.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Explosive Opening Act
No one matches your energy in the first 5 minutes — you shoot the voltage of the room to maximum immediately. You have a natural star quality. However, your brilliance is short-lived. Your body simply cannot keep up with your spirit, and by mid-evening you either freeze up or go fully off the rails. This beautiful, tragic arc is your defining characteristic.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The First Bar Legend
From the first sip you own the room, going full throttle before anyone else. However by the time the group moves to the second bar, you're either frozen in your seat or a permanent resident of the bathroom. The next morning, you discover a camera roll full of extremely unhinged selfies you have zero memory of taking.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: The Detonator of Stiff Atmospheres
Your total absence of inhibitions can instantly transform a tense, formal dinner into a chill hangout. Your pure, unfiltered chaotic energy sometimes demolishes relationship walls that logic couldn't touch, and brings team members closer in seconds. People quietly respect the sheer commitment.

Growth Tip

Stamina & Pacing
Install the concept of "mid-game pacing" into your brain. Don't burn all your energy at bar one. Staying conscious through dessert is the first step toward becoming a true social legend.

Best Match

FOUT (The Drunk Saint)

While you're turning to ash, FOUT quietly brings you water and books you a taxi without anyone noticing. The best caretaker you'll ever have.

Worst Match

LODT (The Drunk Mastermind)

LODT wants to control everything calmly and strategically. Your unpredictable chaos is their biggest nightmare. Expect cold stares and a quiet debrief at work the next day.
