The Drunk Saint (FOUT)

Can drink anyone under the table and still oozes warmth and grace. The all-accepting, ultra-positive goddess that everyone wants at their table — and wants to be.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Hospitality Queen of the Alcohol World
No matter how many drinks you've had, your expression never changes, your warmth never dims. You enjoy every sip, but your primary awareness is always on the happiness of everyone around you. Your presence alone creates an inexplicable sense of ease. No matter how chaotic the night gets, you are the unshakeable anchor.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The All-Affirming Listening Station
Every cringe story, every heavy confession — you receive it all with "I get it, I totally get it" and make it disappear. You also notice the lonely person across the table and fold them into the conversation without making it weird. Your seat is always the most fought-over one.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: Absolute Psychological Safety
In any room you're in, everyone leaves feeling like it was a good night. This is entirely because of your unconditional love and bottomless capacity. You dissolve conflict, ease tension, and make even difficult people feel seen. The team's emotional foundation starts and ends with you.

Growth Tip

【Self-Assertion】
Try stepping down from the sainthood sometimes and making a selfish request. When people see you actually want something, they love you even more.
