Your drunk personality is...

The Clingy Drunk Monster (FOUY)

Can't handle booze but overflows with love. Two drinks in and you're trying to group-hug everyone, before passing out in a state of absolute bliss.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Love Overflow Baby
Your social bonding instinct is off the charts. Alcohol flips the switch on all the "I love you all so much" energy you've been suppressing, and it comes out as hugs, shoulder-grabs, and declarations. Your affection is genuine, unfiltered, and overwhelming. You cannot lie and you cannot hide how you feel. A beautiful disaster.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The Mandatory Cuddle Session
"I love everyone here SO much! This team is EVERYTHING!" — you announce this while side-hugging someone who wasn't expecting it, before passing out contentedly in the corner thirty minutes later. The next morning you remember approximately 40% of who you hugged and immediately want to leave the planet.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: The Instant Wall-Destroyer
Your chaos has a remarkable side effect: the day after your affectionate meltdown, the team atmosphere is somehow warmer and communication is smoother. Your love blasts through walls that professionalism couldn't touch. You're a unique and irreplaceable social catalyst.

Growth Tip

【Personal Space Awareness】
Before going physical, do a quick scan of whether the other person seems genuinely okay with it. Love lands deeper when there's appropriate distance between sender and receiver.

Best Match

· FOUT (The Drunk Saint): FOUT will receive every single hug, every dramatic declaration, and every level of clinginess without flinching. The safest person you can possibly let loose around.

Worst Match

· LODT (The Drunk Mastermind): LODT values personal space and efficiency. Your physical affection overload registers to them as "inefficient risk exposure." Expect a cold stare from across the table.
