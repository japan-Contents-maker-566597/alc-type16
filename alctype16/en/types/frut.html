Your drunk personality is...

The Drunk Hall Monitor (FRUT)

Joins the fun fully and drinks sensibly. Never embarrassing, always present. Shows up to the next day's 9am meeting looking completely unaffected. An inspiration and a mystery.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Vibe-and-Rationality Hybrid Ace
All-in for the party, moderate on the drinks, never loses their dignity, and absolutely nails the next morning. You are an adaptive social genius of the highest order. Wherever you go, people feel like "we're safe with this person around." Your self-management and social ability are in perfect balance.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: Smooth Multi-Tasker
You keep the conversation going, notice what people need, subtly get orders in, and gracefully redirect drunk friends into taxis — all while having genuine fun. The polish and grace of your performance suggests an elite leader in training. You make everyone feel happy without making anyone feel managed.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: Unmatched Coordination and Total Reliability
There are no bad nights with you involved. Your chaos management abilities hint at things to come. Superiors trust you, juniors adore you. You are a full-spectrum diplomat whose presence alone raises the quality floor of any event.

Growth Tip

Unleashing the Chaos
Try dropping the perfect-person suit once in a while and doing something memorably unhinged. When people see you be genuinely vulnerable, trust transforms into something deeper: real love.

Best Match

LRUY (The Unbothered Drinker)

Both are disciplined and socially tuned, so the night stays clean, substantive, and genuinely fun. Two people who actually know how to have a good time responsibly.

Worst Match

LOUY (The Blackout Time Bomb)

LOUY's total chaos quietly triggers your perfectionist side and drains your energy. Do not let yourself get pulled into their orbit.
