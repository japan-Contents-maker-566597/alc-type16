Your drunk personality is...

The Hydration Goblin (LRDY)

Low tolerance, massive regret energy. Goes off every single time, but shows up the next morning with a detailed apology. Secretly obsessed with stealing your water glass.

Core Traits

Core Traits: The Apology-Driven Loveable Disaster
You know your limits. You know what will happen. You go there anyway, every single time. But your morning-after recovery speed and the sincerity of your apologies completely cancel out the damage. You are the most fearless kind of person: you fail, you forget briefly, and you try again.

At a Night Out

At a Night Out: The Great Water Heist
Once the drinks hit, your survival instinct kicks in and you begin silently stealing the water glass of whoever is sitting next to you. "I'm so sorry, I'll make it up to you" is your catchphrase, usually said moments before committing the exact same mistake again. The next morning, you find long emotional texts you sent at 1am and want to move countries.

Strengths & Charm

Strengths & Charm: The Loveable Mess That Keeps the Group Sane
Your earnest imperfection activates the protective instincts of everyone around you. Your incidents become the group's favourite stories, and somehow you function as a natural tension diffuser in stressful team environments. You are loved for being exactly who you are.

Growth Tip

Self-Inventory
Before drinking, check your actual liver capacity like you'd check your phone battery. Your recovery game is elite, but nobody will be mad if you have one peaceful, incident-free night for once.

Best Match

FODY (The 2AM Drunk Crybaby)

Two lightweights with zero dignity, holding each other's hands and crying into water glasses together. The most pure and healing chaos imaginable.

Worst Match

LRUT (The Vibe Curator)

LRUT's meticulously planned event schedule cannot survive your unpredictable meltdowns. You are the biggest scheduling risk they have ever encountered.
